# Quoridor Training Analysis: Wall-Spam Local Minimum

This report documents our investigation into the current training progress (Epoch 83–85), diagnosing the "Wall-Spam" local minimum, evaluating neural network architecture health, and outlining recommendations.

---

## 1. Symptoms & Empirical Findings

### A. 100% Wall Consumption in Every Game
Inspection of self-play trajectories from `data/epoch_085/` showed that:
* **P1 & P2 Wall Usage**: Both Player 1 and Player 2 used **10 out of 10 walls in 100% of analyzed games**.
* **Average Game Length**: ~94.2 moves per game. The first ~20 moves of every game consist entirely of immediate wall dumping before either player focuses on advancing their pawn.

### B. Policy Network Bias Towards Wall Actions
Evaluating the current `quoridor_checkpoint.pth` model across 86 different board states revealed:
* **Zero Pawn Moves in Top Rank**: Across all tested states, **0 out of 86** top predicted actions were pawn moves (actions 0–11). The top ~35 predicted actions were exclusively wall placements (actions 12–139).
* **High Logit Correlation**: Pairwise cosine similarity of policy logits across distinct board states averaged `~0.757`, showing a strong prior favoring wall placement over spatial movement.

### C. Policy Loss vs. Entropy Lock
* **Policy Loss ($\approx 1.7299$) vs. Policy Entropy ($\approx 1.7414$)**: The policy loss is almost identical to the predicted policy's entropy.
* **Interpretation**: The network's KL divergence relative to the MCTS targets is near zero ($D_{KL} \approx 0$), meaning the network has accurately memorized the target distributions generated by MCTS. However, because the MCTS targets are flat/high-entropy wall distributions, the policy network has stalled in learning deeper positional plays.

### D. Neural Architecture & Value Head Health (No Model Collapse)
Unlike the previous collapse where the network output constant scalar values and zero gradients:
* **Value Network Statistics**: Mean `-0.157`, Standard Deviation `0.777`, Min `-0.999`, Max `+0.999`.
* **Conclusion**: The 6-channel spatial CNN + scalar concatenation architecture is healthy. Value predictions are dynamic and non-degenerate. The issue is purely driven by self-play data quality.

---

## 2. Root Cause Analysis

### Shallow MCTS Search in Wide Action Space
* **Configured Simulations**: `NUM_SIMULATIONS = 100` in `run_actor.py` (`40` in `train_alphazero.py`).
* **Branching Factor Problem**: Quoridor has up to ~140 valid actions per turn. Spreading 100 simulations across 140 actions yields $< 1$ visit per action on average.
* **Short-Term Heuristic Trap**: Placing a wall immediately increases the opponent's path length or restricts their movement, creating an immediate short-term heuristic advantage. With only 100 simulations, MCTS cannot look 10+ plies ahead to realize that early wall-dumping does not contribute to a winning pawn trajectory.
* **Feedback Loop**: Shallow MCTS generates wall-heavy target policies $\rightarrow$ Neural network learns to predict wall placements $\rightarrow$ Self-play actors use network priors, further reinforcing wall placements.

---

## 3. Recommended Actions

| Action | Description | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Increase MCTS Simulations** | Set `NUM_SIMULATIONS = 400` (or `800`) in `run_actor.py` | Gives MCTS sufficient search depth to look past immediate wall placements and evaluate long-term pawn progress. |
| **Clear Replay Buffer** | Delete existing `data/` directory and restart training from Epoch 0 | Prevents the model from training on low-quality wall-spam self-play trajectories. |
| **Keep Neural Architecture** | Maintain the 6-channel spatial CNN trunk + scalar FC entry | Architecture is functioning well; no changes needed to network layers. |
| **Fix Diagnostic Metrics** | Wire up `avg_mcts_eval` and `avg_abs_mcts_eval` in `train_alphazero.py` | Currently hardcoded at `0.0`; updating this will allow proper monitoring of search evaluations during training. |
